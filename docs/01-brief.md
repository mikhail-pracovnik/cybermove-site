# 01 — Brief

## The company

- **Brand:** CYBERMOVE (wordmark). Legal/footer name: CYBER MOVE CONSULTING.
- **Tagline:** RU «Двигаем бизнес вперёд.» / EN "We move business forward."
- **What they do:** one partner that connects marketing, brand, AI automation, business development, tender support and legal support into one growth system. Entry point is a Business & Marketing Audit.
- **Method (their own):** Diagnosis → Core → System → Growth. The "Core" (Ядро) = product, audience, positioning, value, differentiation, offer, message, brand DNA. Nothing is scaled until the core is defined.
- **Stance on AI:** "AI should work, not impress." Automate a process that already works, not the wrong process faster. AI is a tool, not the product.
- **Geography:** projects and business ties in Europe, Central Asia and APAC (13 cities — see `content/data/geography.json`). Phone is Kazakhstan (+7 701…), so the primary market is Kazakhstan / CIS, with a strong Ukrainian and European track record.
- **Track record:** 30+ brands and projects (see `content/data/projects.json`) — from a world boxing champion's personal brand and Ukrainian Fashion Week to industrial engineering distributors in Kazakhstan.
- **Team:** 6 people (see `content/data/team.json`).

## Audience

Owners, founders and C-level of small and mid-size companies (retail, HoReCa, B2B/industrial, experts/personal brands) in Kazakhstan, CIS and Europe. They read on the phone, between meetings. They are tired of agencies that sell "more marketing". They need to see in 10 seconds: *who you are, what you do, for whom you have done it, how to start.*

## Goal of the site

1. Explain the offer in one screen.
2. Prove it with names (projects) and people (team).
3. Convert: the single primary action is **"Start with an audit" → Contact** (WhatsApp or form).

## Tone of voice

- Calm, precise, confident. Short sentences. No hype, no exclamation marks, no "revolutionary", "unique", "best-in-class".
- Russian must be literate, natural business Russian — not translated English, not anglicism soup. English must be natural, not translated Russian.
- Headlines state a thought; body text explains in 1–2 sentences. Never more than ~40 words in a paragraph.

## What was wrong with the old site (do the opposite)

Full audit: `reference/old-site-audit.md`. In short:

1. Home page overloaded with text: 15+ sections, 12 "pain" quotes, 34 client cards, 12 AI bullets, 10 metrics, 13 cities — all on one page.
2. Custom animated cursor lagging far behind the real one.
3. Images off-style and inconsistent.
4. RU/EN mixed in the same page ("YOU DON'T NEED MORE MARKETING" above Russian text); awkward, non-literary phrasing ("работают вразнос", "Начинаем не с услуги — с задачи" with no context).
5. Inconsistent naming (CYBERMOVE / CYBER MOVE / CYBER MOVE CONSULTING).
6. "View case" buttons leading nowhere real; category labels presented as clients ("E-COMMERCE ПРОЕКТЫ").
7. Only one contact channel, visually buried.

## What was good and should be kept (in spirit)

- **The Team page**: large portrait, focus line, name, role, 1–2 sentence bio, 4 competence tags, direct CTA. Keep this structure; restyle it to the new design system.
- The methodology (Core → System → Growth) and the audit as an entry offer.
- The project list itself (it is the richest asset — just present it honestly and calmly).
